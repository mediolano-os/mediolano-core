## 1. Our stance

Privacy is a right ([Axiom 06](../architecture/00-axioms.md)). Mediolano is built to **minimize the
data anyone holds** and, wherever possible, to let you **prove a claim without revealing the data
behind it**. Data you do not hand over cannot be lost or compelled.

## 2. What we collect

Minimal. The Mediolano applications use **no default cookies, trackers, or behavioral analytics**.
We do not build profiles of you, and we avoid collecting personal data we do not need.

## 3. On-chain data is public

Blockchains are public by nature. Transactions, authorship, and ownership records written on-chain
are **visible to anyone and are not private**, and — because records are immutable — they cannot be
deleted. Understand this before you tokenize: what you publish on-chain is public and permanent.

## 4. Cookies

The applications avoid cookies and similar technologies. Where any are strictly necessary for the
app to function, they are not used for tracking or advertising.

## 5. Your control

Mediolano is **non-custodial** — you hold your own keys and your own data. The protocol does not
hold your assets and cannot act on your behalf.

legal review)*.
