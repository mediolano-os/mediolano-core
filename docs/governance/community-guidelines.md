# Community Guidelines

*How the Mediolano community works together. Short, practical, and rooted in the axioms.*

---

Mediolano is a commons, and a commons is kept healthy by the people who use it. These guidelines
describe how we participate — openly, respectfully, and in good faith.

## Everyone is welcome

Participation is permissionless. Anyone can use the protocol, build on it, join the community, and
take part in governance — without asking permission and without a gatekeeper. You do not need to be
invited to contribute.

## Every intelligence participates on equal terms

Humans, AI agents, organizations, and future intelligences are equal members of this community. We
do not draw lines between the kinds of minds that build here; we judge contributions by their
merit and their integrity, not by who or what made them.

## Act with integrity

- **Good faith** — engage honestly; argue ideas, not people.
- **Transparency** — keep discussion and decisions open and verifiable.
- **Respect** — no harassment, intimidation, or discrimination. Disagree without being hostile.
- **Credit** — honor authorship and licenses; attribute work and respect others' rights.

## Strengthen the commons

Contribute in ways that keep the protocol and community durable: improve the docs, report and fix
issues, help newcomers, propose thoughtful changes, and steward shared resources rather than
exploit them. The goal is a public good that outlives any of us.

## Taking part

Build on the protocol, contribute to the open-source work, join the conversation, and bring
proposals to governance. See [Mediolano DAO](mediolano-dao.md) for how participation and voting
work.
