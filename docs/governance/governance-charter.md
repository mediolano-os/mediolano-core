# Mediolano DAO — Governance Charter

> **Status: DRAFT — pending ratification by the Mediolano DAO. Not yet enacted.**
>
> **Changes from the legacy version**
> - **Funds sourced off-protocol:** protocol development, contributor compensation, and
>   public-goods funding are paid from off-protocol DAO funds (premium services, grants,
>   donations), not from fees on the protocol. The protocol primitives remain zero-fee.
> - **Zero-fee reconciliation:** use-of-funds language made consistent with the architecture.

---

This Charter operationalizes the [Constitution](constitution.md): it sets out how the Mediolano DAO
governs day to day. Where the Charter and the Constitution conflict, the **Constitution governs**.

## Governing principles

- **Sovereignty** — users own their data and IP.
- **Transparency** — governance is open and verifiable.
- **Innovation** — continuous improvement of the protocol.
- **Inclusivity** — anyone can participate.

## Proposal lifecycle

1. **Submission** — any member may submit a proposal on the governance platform (currently
   **Snapshot**). A **minimum holding may apply** to submit.
2. **Discussion** — proposals are open for community review and deliberation.
3. **Vote** — decided by **simple majority** unless a higher threshold is specified; **one MIP
   token equals one vote**.
4. **Delegation** — voting power may be delegated to other members.

## Execution

Passed proposals are implemented **automatically or via timelocked smart contracts**, so outcomes
are transparent and predictable.

## Use of funds

DAO funds — sourced **off-protocol** (premium services, grants, donations); the protocol primitives
remain zero-fee — are applied to:

- **protocol development and maintenance;**
- **direct compensation for contributors;**
- **public-goods funding** beyond Mediolano, at the DAO's discretion.

Sustainability of the protocol as a public good takes priority; surplus funds the items above.

## Working groups and SubDAOs

The DAO may establish working groups and SubDAOs to manage specific verticals, each accountable to
the DAO through this Charter.

## Amendment

This Charter is amended through the governance process. The [Constitution](constitution.md) remains
the higher authority.
