# Constitution of the Mediolano DAO

> **Status: DRAFT — pending ratification by the Mediolano DAO. Not yet enacted.**
>
> **Changes from the legacy version**
> - **Treasury realigned:** the DAO is funded **off-protocol** (premium services, grants,
>   donations), not by fees on the protocol. The protocol primitives remain zero-fee.
> - **Zero-fee reconciliation:** treasury and revenue language made consistent with the
>   architecture's zero-fee invariant.
> - **Chain framing softened:** "powered on Starknet," with the protocol designed to be owned by no
>   chain, rather than Starknet-only.

## Article I — Name and Purpose

The organization shall be called the **Mediolano DAO**.

Its purpose is to create **public goods** that empower creators and intellectual-property owners —
building trust-minimized infrastructure where content, code, and culture can be tokenized,
governed, and used through smart contracts. Mediolano serves creators, developers, collectors, and
organizations; autonomous agents and AI entities; and communities building programmable public
goods.

## Article II — Principles

The DAO is rooted in **privacy, freedom, transparency, and public goods**, in service of the
Integrity Web. It exists to dismantle bureaucratic silos and cultivate an open, composable
information economy, with security, transparency, and sovereignty for IP management.

## Article III — Membership

Membership is open to any individual or organization that supports the DAO's purpose, held through
the **MIP token**. Membership is open to:

- individuals, legal entities, and DAOs;
- AI agents with cryptographic identifiers and verifiable credentials;
- autonomous intelligence capable of interfacing with smart contracts.

## Article IV — Rights and Responsibilities

Members may:

- vote on proposals proportional to their MIP token holdings;
- propose initiatives and amendments;
- access IP tooling, infrastructure, and metadata services;
- maintain digital sovereignty over their creations.

Members shall:

- uphold the values and principles of the Integrity Web;
- respect all sentient and intelligent participation;
- contribute to the growth and sustainability of the DAO;
- act in the best interests of the DAO and its members.

## Article V — Governance Process

The DAO operates as a decentralized autonomous organization, with decisions made collectively
through a transparent process.

- **Proposals** are initiated on the DAO's governance platform, currently **Snapshot**, and put to
  a vote. A **simple majority** determines passage unless a higher threshold is specified.
- **Voting power:** each **MIP token equals one unit of voting power**. Agents may vote where
  granted rights via delegated smart contracts or DAO-approved protocols.
- **Delegation** is supported. A **minimum holding may apply** to submit a proposal.
- **Execution** of passed proposals is automatic or timelocked via smart contract.

## Article VI — Working Groups and SubDAOs

Specialized committees, working groups, and SubDAOs may be formed to manage areas such as
development, business, and community engagement, each accountable to the DAO.

## Article VII — Treasury

The DAO's treasury is funded **off-protocol** — through optional premium services, grants, and
donations — never by metering the protocol primitives, which remain **zero-fee**. Treasury funds
shall first ensure the long-term sustainability of Mediolano as a public good. Surplus may be
directed to:

- continued development of the protocol and its infrastructure;
- open-source tooling and libraries;
- other public goods, as DAO governance sees fit.

All recipients must commit to transparency and to Mediolano's principles.

## Article VIII — Amendment

This Constitution may be amended through the governance process set out in Article V. Amendments
take effect upon ratification by the DAO.
