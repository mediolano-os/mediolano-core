# Mediolano

*Public-goods tokenization for the Integrity Web.*

---

Intellectual property is the inheritance of culture — everything humans and machines imagine,
compose, discover, and make. That inheritance should be free to create, permanent once made, and
owned by its creators. Not licensed back to them by platforms. Not held hostage by a database, a
gateway, or a border. We are building the commons where authorship is proven, protected, and
portable — forever.

This is Mediolano.

## Creativity is integrity

Knowledge, art, and invention are tokenized as public goods. Ideas stay free; their integrity is
preserved forever. To create is to leave a record that cannot be erased.

## Authorship is permanent

Every record is tamper-proof. Every action is verifiable. What is proven is true, and truth does
not expire. Your authorship is not a claim you defend — it is a fact anyone can check.

## No gatekeepers. No fees.

Participation is permissionless. No authority grants you entry; no intermediary takes a cut. The
primitives are a zero-fee commons, and a commons cannot meter the people it belongs to.

## Privacy is sovereignty

Information belongs to its creator. Prove what you must without revealing what you need not.
Privacy is not secrecy — it is the right to remain the author of your own data.

## For every intelligence

Humans, AI agents, and the intelligences not yet born share the same right to participate. The
commons makes no distinction between the minds that build on it.

## Owned by no chain

Verifiable trust is a role, not a place. Identity and the work itself outlive any single chain.
What you make is anchored to proof, not trapped by infrastructure — and you always keep a way out.

## A commons, forever

Infrastructure belongs to everyone. Proof systems, identity, and tokenization are shared ground —
the trust backbone of a digital civilization that no one owns and no one can shut down.
